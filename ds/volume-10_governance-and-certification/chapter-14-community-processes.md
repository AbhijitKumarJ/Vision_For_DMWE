# Chapter 14: Community Processes

**Volume X — Ecosystem Governance, Compliance & Certification (EGCC) · Working Draft v0.1**

## 14.1 Purpose

Defines collaborative development. Transparency and documented decision-making are emphasized.

## 14.2 Community Topics

- Working groups.
- Public reviews.
- Issue tracking.
- Proposal templates.
- Meeting procedures.
- Voting.
- Consensus building.

## 14.3 Working Groups

- Focused groups per topic (Chapter 2).
- Open membership.
- Documented scope.

## 14.4 Public Reviews

- Review periods for drafts (Chapter 3).
- All feedback is considered.
- Resolution summaries are public.

## 14.5 Issue Tracking

- Public issue tracker.
- Issues are categorized.
- Resolution is recorded.

## 14.6 Proposal Templates

- Standard template (Chapter 3).
- Requires scope, rationale, impact.
- Templates are public.

## 14.7 Meeting Procedures

- Open meetings.
- Minutes are public.
- Decisions are recorded.

## 14.8 Voting

- Defined voting rules.
- Quorum requirements stated.
- Votes are public.

## 14.9 Consensus Building

- Consensus is preferred over voting.
- Dissenting views are documented.
- Appeal process exists.

## 14.10 Code of Conduct

- A code of conduct governs participation.
- Violations are handled fairly.
- Enforcement is documented.

## 14.11 Conformance

A conformant community MUST follow transparent processes with documented decisions.
