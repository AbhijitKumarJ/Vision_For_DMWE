# Chapter 10: Developer & Vendor Programs

**Volume X — Ecosystem Governance, Compliance & Certification (EGCC) · Working Draft v0.1**

## 10.1 Purpose

Describes participation pathways. Responsibilities and privileges are defined for each.

## 10.2 Program Types

- Individual developer.
- Academic research.
- Startup.
- Commercial vendor.
- Hardware manufacturer.
- Certification laboratory.
- Open-source contributor.

## 10.3 Individual Developer

- Access to SDK and docs (Volume IX).
- Community participation (Chapter 14).
- Publish experimental applications.

## 10.4 Academic Research

- Research access (Volume VII, Ch 11).
- Experimental features (Chapter 4).
- Publication partnerships (Chapter 15).

## 10.5 Startup

- Early certification pathways.
- Startup support programs.
- Fast-track tooling access.

## 10.6 Commercial Vendor

- Full certification (Chapter 6).
- Product listing in registries (Chapter 11).
- Participation in committees.

## 10.7 Hardware Manufacturer

- Hardware certification (Volume III).
- Module Descriptor compliance (Volume III, Ch 4).
- Sample hardware contributions.

## 10.8 Certification Laboratory

- Accredited by the Certification Committee (Chapter 2).
- Runs conformance suites (Chapter 8).
- Maintains audit records.

## 10.9 Open-Source Contributor

- Contributes to reference implementations (Chapter 9).
- Submits proposals (Chapter 14).
- Contributor agreements required (Chapter 13).

## 10.10 Responsibilities

- All participants follow the code of conduct.
- All participants honor licensing (Chapter 13).
- All participants respect security processes (Chapter 12).

## 10.11 Conformance

A conformant program MUST define responsibilities and privileges for each participant type.
