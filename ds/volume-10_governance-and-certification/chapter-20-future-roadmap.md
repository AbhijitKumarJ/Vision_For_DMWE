# Chapter 20: Future Roadmap

**Volume X — Ecosystem Governance, Compliance & Certification (EGCC) · Working Draft v0.1**

## 20.1 Purpose

Concludes the specification set by outlining long-term aspirations. This chapter frames DMWE as a living standard rather than a static publication.

## 20.2 Roadmap Topics

- Planned specification revisions.
- Emerging research topics.
- Experimental extensions.
- Cross-standard collaboration.
- International adoption.
- Sustainability initiatives.

## 20.3 Planned Specification Revisions

- Revisions follow the lifecycle (Chapter 3).
- Priorities come from metrics (Chapter 19).
- Roadmap is public.

## 20.4 Emerging Research Topics

- Multimodal AI.
- Spatial computing at scale.
- Ambient intelligence.
- Novel sensing.
- Research feeds standards (Chapter 16).

## 20.5 Experimental Extensions

- Opt-in experiments (Chapter 4).
- Evaluated for adoption.
- Non-certified until stable.

## 20.6 Cross-Standard Collaboration

- Matter, Thread, Bluetooth, USB (Volume VIII, Ch 15).
- Accessibility and AI standards.
- Interoperability agreements.

## 20.7 International Adoption

- Localization of standards and tools.
- Regional certification.
- Global governance participation.

## 20.8 Sustainability Initiatives

- Energy efficiency goals (Chapter 16).
- Repairability and longevity.
- Circular lifecycle (Volume III, Ch 11).

## 20.9 The Living Standards Ecosystem

DMWE is a complete standards ecosystem:

- Reference Architecture Repository.
- Reference Test Repository.
- Capability Registry.
- Interaction Registry.
- Ontology & Knowledge Repository.
- Protocol Registry.
- Developer Portal.
- Conformance Dashboard.

This provides a sustainable path for research, implementation, certification, and community-driven evolution.

## 20.10 Conclusion

The ten volumes together define a coherent, interoperable, and evolvable ecosystem:

- Vision (I).
- Architecture (II).
- Hardware (III, 3A).
- MeshOS (IV).
- Capabilities (V).
- Human Interaction (VI).
- Cognitive Intelligence (VII).
- Protocols (VIII).
- Application Framework (IX).
- Governance (X).

## 20.11 Conformance

A conformant ecosystem MUST maintain a public roadmap aligned with this vision.
