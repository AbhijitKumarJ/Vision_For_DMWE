# Chapter 3: Specification Lifecycle

**Volume X — Ecosystem Governance, Compliance & Certification (EGCC) · Working Draft v0.1**

## 3.1 Purpose

Defines how standards evolve.

## 3.2 Lifecycle Stages

```text
Proposal
    ↓
Discussion
    ↓
Draft
    ↓
Review
    ↓
Public Comment
    ↓
Revision
    ↓
Approval
    ↓
Publication
    ↓
Maintenance
    ↓
Retirement
```

Each stage has documented entry and exit criteria.

## 3.3 Proposal

- Change or new standard proposed.
- Proposal template followed (Chapter 14).
- Entry: clear scope and rationale.

## 3.4 Discussion

- Committee evaluates the proposal.
- Alternatives considered.
- Exit: decision to draft or reject.

## 3.5 Draft

- Technical content developed.
- Normative language enforced.
- Cross-references verified.

## 3.6 Review

- Internal technical review.
- Conformance impact assessed.
- Exit: readiness for public comment.

## 3.7 Public Comment

- Community review period.
- Feedback collected and resolved.
- Exit: comment resolution summary.

## 3.8 Revision

- Comments incorporated.
- Final editorial review.
- Exit: ready for approval.

## 3.9 Approval

- Committee votes.
- Consensus required (Chapter 14).
- Exit: approved status.

## 3.10 Publication

- Official release.
- Machine-readable metadata published.
- Versioned per Chapter 4.

## 3.11 Maintenance

- Errata and clarifications.
- Minor revisions.
- Compatibility preserved.

## 3.12 Retirement

- Deprecation announced.
- Migration guidance provided (Chapter 17).
- Removal after the deprecation window.

## 3.13 Conformance

A conformant standards body MUST follow the lifecycle with documented entry and exit criteria.
