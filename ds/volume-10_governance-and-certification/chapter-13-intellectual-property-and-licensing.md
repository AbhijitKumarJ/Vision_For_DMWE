# Chapter 13: Intellectual Property & Licensing

**Volume X — Ecosystem Governance, Compliance & Certification (EGCC) · Working Draft v0.1**

## 13.1 Purpose

Discusses legal considerations. This chapter should be developed with legal counsel and tailored to the chosen governance model (e.g., foundation, consortium, or standards organization).

## 13.2 Legal Topics

- Specification licensing.
- Reference implementation licensing.
- Patent disclosures.
- Trademark usage.
- Certification marks.
- Contributor agreements.

## 13.3 Specification Licensing

- Specifications are openly licensed.
- License terms defined by the governing body.
- Licenses permit implementation.

## 13.4 Reference Implementation Licensing

- Reference implementations are open source.
- Contribution terms defined.
- License compatible with commercial use.

## 13.5 Patent Disclosures

- Participants disclose essential patents.
- FRAND terms apply where applicable.
- Disclosures are public.

## 13.6 Trademark Usage

- DMWE marks are protected.
- Usage guidelines published.
- Misuse is actionable.

## 13.7 Certification Marks

- Marks indicate certification (Chapter 6).
- Marks are licensed to certified entities.
- Marks MUST NOT be used without certification.

## 13.8 Contributor Agreements

- Contributors assign or license rights.
- Agreements are transparent.
- Governance follows the chosen model.

## 13.9 Conformance

A conformant ecosystem MUST publish licensing terms, honor patents, protect marks, and require contributor agreements.
