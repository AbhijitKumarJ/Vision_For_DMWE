# Chapter 11: Ecosystem Registry

**Volume X — Ecosystem Governance, Compliance & Certification (EGCC) · Working Draft v0.1**

## 11.1 Purpose

Defines centralized or federated registries. Registry synchronization and governance are described.

## 11.2 Registry Contents

- Certified devices.
- Certified applications.
- Registered capabilities.
- Approved protocol profiles.
- Trusted manufacturers.
- Plugin catalogs.

## 11.3 Certified Devices

- List of certified modules and devices.
- Compliance level declared (Chapter 5).
- Certification status tracked.

## 11.4 Certified Applications

- List of certified applications (Volume IX, Ch 20).
- Certification profile recorded.
- Version history maintained.

## 11.5 Registered Capabilities

- Canonical capability definitions (Volume V).
- Version histories.
- Substitution relationships (Capability Graph).

## 11.6 Approved Protocol Profiles

- Approved profiles (Volume VIII, Ch 18).
- Transport adapter registrations.
- Extension identifiers (Volume VIII, Ch 6).

## 11.7 Trusted Manufacturers

- Verified manufacturer identities.
- Trust anchors maintained (Chapter 12).
- Revocation handling defined.

## 11.8 Plugin Catalogs

- Extension catalog (Volume IX, Ch 18).
- Versioned and signed.
- Compatibility checked.

## 11.9 Registry Governance

- Registries are committee-governed.
- Entries are validated and auditable.
- Registries may be centralized or federated.

## 11.10 Synchronization

- Federated registries sync consistently.
- Conflicts resolve deterministically (Volume VIII, Ch 9).
- Sync is consent-aware.

## 11.11 Conformance

A conformant registry MUST validate, version, and govern its entries per this chapter.
