# Chapter 8: Accessibility & Inclusion

**Volume XII — Reference Applications · Working Draft v0.1**

## 8.1 Phantom Braille / Ghost Scroll

**Problem:** Reading and navigation for low-vision or hands-occupied scenarios.

- **Capabilities:** `Micro Gesture`, `Gaze`, `Audio Output`.
- **Grammar:** `Jaw Clench` or `Prolonged Blink` → Next Page / Select.
- **Context:** Low vision or hands-occupied scenarios.
- **Experience:** Fully eyes- or hands-free interaction.

## 8.2 Bio-Token MFA

**Problem:** Friction in authentication.

- **Capabilities:** `Authentication` (EMG signature).
- **Grammar:** Personalized micro-flex sequence.
- **Context:** Login attempt.
- **Experience:** Instant, passwordless, zero-friction approval.

## 8.3 Accessibility Requirements

- Alternative modalities MUST be available (Volume VI, Ch 14).
- Interactions work eyes-free or hands-free.
- Accessibility is verified in certification (Volume X, Ch 6).

## 8.4 Common Patterns

- Biometric signatures replace passwords.
- Micro-gestures and gaze replace touch.
- Audio output replaces visual feedback.

## 8.5 Conformance

These applications demonstrate conformant accessibility patterns: alternative modalities and friction-free inclusion.
