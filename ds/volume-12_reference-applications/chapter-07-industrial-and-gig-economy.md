# Chapter 7: Industrial & Gig Economy

**Volume XII — Reference Applications · Working Draft v0.1**

## 7.1 Hands-Free Logistics

**Problem:** Workers juggle boxes and scanners.

- **Capabilities:** `Vision`, `Object Recognition`, `Haptic`.
- **Grammar:** Auto-scan on lift → Green/Red haptic confirmation.
- **Context:** Delivery/warehouse mode.
- **Fusion:** Ring camera + Necklace haptics + Truck IBN.
- **Experience:** Never stop to scan.

## 7.2 Smart Wheel Assist

**Problem:** Eyes off the road for phone interaction.

- **Capabilities:** `Gesture`, `Scalar`.
- **Grammar:** Steering-wheel slide (capacitive cover) + double-tap.
- **Context:** Driving mode (safety restrictions).
- **Experience:** Keep hands on the wheel, eyes on the road.

## 7.3 Safety Requirements

- Driving mode MUST apply safety filters (Volume VI, Ch 15).
- Distracting output is suppressed.
- Industrial modes follow the Industrial profile (Volume VIII, Ch 18).

## 7.4 Common Patterns

- Haptic confirmation replaces visual scanning.
- Vehicle/industrial context gates interactions.
- High-reliability communication (Volume VIII, Ch 12).

## 7.5 Conformance

These applications demonstrate conformant industrial patterns: hands-free operation, safety gating, and reliable haptics.
