# Chapter 9: Fitness, Health & Outdoors

**Volume XII — Reference Applications · Working Draft v0.1**

## 9.1 Flow State

**Problem:** Staying in flow while running or cycling without watch glances.

- **Capabilities:** `Biometric`, `Scalar`, `Gesture`.
- **Grammar:** `Double Finger Tap` → Speak current pace.
- **Context:** Running/cycling (high macro movement).
- **Experience:** Stay in flow; no watch glances needed.

## 9.2 Invisible Leash

**Problem:** Hands-free drone control during outdoor activity.

- **Capabilities:** `Gesture`, `Spatial`.
- **Grammar:** Palm-up raise → Increase altitude; "Come here" gesture.
- **Context:** Outdoor activity.
- **Experience:** Hands-free drone following while hiking or snowboarding.

## 9.3 Fitness Requirements

- High-movement contexts require robust gesture recognition.
- Audio output is preferred during exertion.
- Biometric data follows health privacy rules (Volume VII, Ch 15).

## 9.4 Common Patterns

- Gesture replaces touch during activity.
- Spatial awareness enables device control.
- Continuous scalar input tracks effort.

## 9.5 Conformance

These applications demonstrate conformant fitness patterns: robust gestures and activity-aware interaction.
