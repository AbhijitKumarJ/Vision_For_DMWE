# Chapter 13: Communication & Social

**Volume XII — Reference Applications · Working Draft v0.1**

## 13.1 Pocket Beam

**Problem:** Awkward QR codes or phone fumbling at events.

- **Capabilities:** `Gesture`, `Secure Transfer`, `Authentication`.
- **Grammar:** `Thumb_Swipe` in pocket → Beam V-Card.
- **Context:** Conversation detected (proximity + audio) → ready mode.
- **Experience:** Exchange contacts discreetly while maintaining eye contact.

## 13.2 Discreet Swipe

**Problem:** Using dating apps in public feels awkward.

- **Capabilities:** `Micro Gesture`, `Scalar`.
- **Grammar:** `Thumb_Flick_Left/Right` on ring → Swipe.
- **Context:** Public space → screen-off, audio-bio mode.
- **Experience:** Browse and interact completely blind and discreetly.

## 13.3 Social Requirements

- Secure transfer MUST be authenticated (Volume VIII, Ch 13).
- Contact data follows user consent (Volume VII, Ch 15).
- Screen-off modes preserve privacy.

## 13.4 Common Patterns

- Micro-gestures replace visible touch.
- Context triggers ready modes.
- Discreet interaction preserves social presence.

## 13.5 Conformance

These applications demonstrate conformant social patterns: discreet interaction and secure transfer.
