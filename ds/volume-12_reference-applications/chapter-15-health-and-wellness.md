# Chapter 15: Health & Wellness

**Volume XII — Reference Applications · Working Draft v0.1**

## 15.1 Digital Twin Medic — Rehab

**Problem:** Personalized physical therapy with guidance.

- **Capabilities:** `Motion Tracking`, `Haptic Guidance`, `Biometric`.
- **Grammar:** Guided micro-movements with real-time haptic correction.
- **Context:** Rehabilitation session.
- **Experience:** Personalized physical therapy with overlaid guidance and progress tracking.

## 15.2 Symbiosis — Focus Guardian

**Problem:** Environmental support for mental state.

- **Capabilities:** `Cognitive State`, `Environmental Control`.
- **Grammar:** Rising stress (EEG/HRV) → automatic focus mode (dim lights, noise cancel).
- **Context:** Work vs relaxation.
- **Experience:** The environment subtly supports mental state.

## 15.3 Health Requirements

- Health data follows the Medical profile (Volume VIII, Ch 18).
- Biometric data is privacy-sensitive (Volume VII, Ch 15).
- Automatic changes MUST be reversible and explainable.

## 15.4 Common Patterns

- Haptic guidance provides real-time correction.
- Cognitive state drives adaptation.
- Progress is tracked in the Digital Twin.

## 15.5 Conformance

These applications demonstrate conformant health patterns: therapeutic guidance and state-aware adaptation.
