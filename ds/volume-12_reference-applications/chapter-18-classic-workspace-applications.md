## 18.3 Mouse Replacement

- **Capabilities:** `Precision Pointer`, `Selection`.
- **Grammar:** `Point` + `Tap` → click; `Point` + `Hold` + `Move` → drag.
- **Context:** Desktop active.
- **Experience:** Replace mouse with wearable pointing.

## 18.4 Spatial Desktop

- **Capabilities:** `Spatial`, `Projection`.
- **Grammar:** Gesture placement of windows in space.
- **Context:** Workspace mode.
- **Experience:** Multiple virtual displays around the user.

## 18.5 Air Keyboard

- **Capabilities:** `Air Typing`, `Gesture`.
- **Grammar:** Finger taps in air → keystrokes.
- **Experience:** Type without a physical keyboard.

## 18.6 Silent Dictation

- **Capabilities:** `Speech`, `Text Generation`.
- **Grammar:** Whispered speech → text.
- **Experience:** Quiet, accurate dictation.

## 18.7 Mixed Reality Workspace

- **Capabilities:** `Layered Spatial`, `Projection`.
- **Grammar:** Objects anchored and manipulated in space.
- **Experience:** Seamless physical + virtual workspace.

## 18.8 Meeting Assistant

- **Capabilities:** `Speech`, `Summarization`, `Context`.
- **Grammar:** Natural-language requests.
- **Experience:** Notes, summaries, and action items captured automatically.

## 18.9 Developer Console

- **Capabilities:** `Terminal`, `Input`, `Projection`.
- **Experience:** Code and debug through wearable input/output.

## 18.10 Presentation Controller

- **Capabilities:** `Precision Pointer`, `Navigation`.
- **Experience:** Slides and laser pointer via gestures (extends Chapter 4).

## 18.11 Industrial Maintenance

- **Capabilities:** `Vision`, `AR Overlay`, `Diagnostics`.
- **Experience:** Guided maintenance with overlaid instructions.

## 18.12 Medical Assistance

- **Capabilities:** `Biometric`, `Telemetry`, `Alerts`.
- **Experience:** Hands-free clinical support (extends Chapter 15).

## 18.13 Robot Control

- **Capabilities:** `Spatial`, `Gesture`.
- **Experience:** Intuitive robot manipulation.

## 18.14 Drone Controller

- **Capabilities:** `Gesture`, `Spatial`.
- **Experience:** Drone control through body movement (extends Chapter 17).

## 18.15 Smart Home

- **Capabilities:** `Semantic Gesture`, `IoT Control`.
- **Experience:** Environment control (extends Chapter 12).

## 18.16 Vehicle Dashboard

- **Capabilities:** `Gesture`, `Spatial Audio`, `Vehicle Integration`.
- **Experience:** In-car control with safety filters (extends Chapter 17).

## 18.17 Conformance

Classic applications demonstrate the foundational use cases; behavior follows the normative volumes.
