# Chapter 11: Advanced & Futuristic

**Volume XII — Reference Applications · Working Draft v0.1**

## 11.1 Digital Twin Medic

**Problem:** Sterile, hands-free overlays in surgical/repair environments.

- **Capabilities:** `Gaze`, `Projection`, `Layered Spatial`.
- **Grammar:** Look + foot pedal scroll through hologram layers.
- **Context:** Surgical/repair environment.
- **Experience:** Sterile, hands-free overlays aligned to physical objects.

## 11.2 Symbiosis

**Problem:** Environmental adaptation to subconscious state.

- **Capabilities:** `Neural / Biometric`, `IoT Control`.
- **Grammar:** Subconscious state → automatic environment changes.
- **Context:** Stress detected → cool lighting, white noise.
- **Experience:** The environment responds before conscious action.

## 11.3 Advanced Requirements

- Medical environments follow the Medical profile (Volume VIII, Ch 18).
- Biometric state changes are privacy-sensitive (Volume VII, Ch 15).
- Automatic changes MUST be reversible and explainable (Volume VII, Ch 10).

## 11.4 Common Patterns

- Layered spatial information aligned to objects.
- Subconscious signals drive adaptation.
- Privacy governs biometric data.

## 11.5 Conformance

These applications demonstrate forward-looking patterns within conformance boundaries.
