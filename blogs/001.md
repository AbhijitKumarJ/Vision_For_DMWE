## Request

now let us hone in on the wearables with specific designs that maximizes usability in terms of number of inputs, ease of carry, modalities, discreet and continuous input. Let us list out best candidates and possible imagined candidates for future innovations

## Response

**Best Current & Emerging Candidates**

*   **EMG Wristbands (e.g., Meta/CTRL-labs):** 
    *   *Why:* Reads neural signals to muscles. Extremely high input (tracks individual finger micro-movements), fully discreet (hands can be in pockets), continuous control, effortless carry.
*   **Smart Rings (with IMU & Touch):** 
    *   *Why:* Maximum ease of carry and discreetness. Supports continuous scrolling via thumb-rubs, tap gestures, and spatial pointing. 
*   **Smart Glasses (with Eye-Tracking & Frame Sensors):** 
    *   *Why:* Multimodal (gaze, voice, capacitive frame swipes). Highly discreet (looks like normal glasses), continuous hands-free navigation.
*   **Advanced Hearables:** 
    *   *Why:* Unbeatable ease of carry. Modalities include voice, head-tracking (nods/shakes), and jaw-movement/teeth-click detection for totally silent, discreet triggers.

**Imagined Future Candidates (Innovations)**

*   **Subvocalization Patches/Chokers:** 
    *   *Concept:* Wearables that read throat muscle signals to transcribe silent speech. 
    *   *Usability:* Infinite inputs (entire vocabulary), completely discreet (no sound, no hand movement), continuous text/command entry.
*   **E-Textile "Smart Pockets":** 
    *   *Concept:* Capacitive, pressure-sensitive threads woven invisibly into jacket/pants pockets. 
    *   *Usability:* High input (multi-touch trackpad), peak discreetness (hands naturally rest in pockets), zero extra carry weight.
*   **Epidermal Sensors (Smart Tattoos/Stickers):** 
    *   *Concept:* Ultra-thin temporary sensors applied to the wrist or hand reading tendon friction or acoustic bone vibrations.
    *   *Usability:* Zero carry effort, invisible, translates complex hand gestures into continuous, high-fidelity inputs.
*   **Smart Contact Lenses:** 
    *   *Concept:* Micro-computing lenses with invisible gaze-tracking and blink-sequence detection. 
    *   *Usability:* The ultimate discreet, hands-free, continuous cursor control.
*   **Ear-EEG (In-Ear Neural Interfaces):** 
    *   *Concept:* Earbuds equipped with brain-computer interface sensors to read intent. 
    *   *Usability:* Zero physical movement required, perfectly discreet, multimodal (combines audio, voice, and thought commands).
