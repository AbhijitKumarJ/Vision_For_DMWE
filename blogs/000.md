## Request

Please list out all the various types of controllers known grouped by category of purpose, form, interaction modalities, wearability. This is in context of controllers for digital machines and software like mouse,keyboard, remote, joystick, scratchpad, rings, bands, options on glasses, hearables, pen/necklace microphone/cameras etc

## Response

Here is the categorization of digital controllers, kept concise and complete:

**1. By Purpose**
*   **Navigation/Pointing:** Mouse, trackpad, trackball, 3D mouse.
*   **Data/Text Entry:** Keyboard, keypad, chorded keyboard.
*   **Gaming/Simulation:** Gamepad, joystick, steering wheel, HOTAS, VR wands, dance pads.
*   **Creative/Professional:** Graphics tablet (scratchpad/digitizer), stylus, MIDI controller, DJ deck, editing dials (e.g., Surface Dial).
*   **Media/Home Control:** Remote control, presentation clicker.
*   **Accessibility:** Sip-and-puff, foot switches, eye-trackers.

**2. By Form**
*   **Desktop/Surface:** Keyboards, mice, trackpads, drawing tablets.
*   **Handheld:** Remotes, gamepads, VR wands, smartphones.
*   **Pen-like:** Digital pen, stylus.
*   **Jewelry/Accessories:** Smart rings, wristbands, smart necklaces, pendant cameras/mics.
*   **Apparel/Soft Goods:** Smart gloves, haptic vests, smart shoes.
*   **Headgear:** Smart glasses (frames), hearables (earbuds), AR/VR headsets, headbands.
*   **Environmental:** Floor mats, external depth cameras (e.g., Kinect).

**3. By Interaction Modalities**
*   **Mechanical/Tactile:** Pressing buttons/keys, flicking switches, turning dials, pushing joysticks.
*   **Touch/Capacitive:** Swiping/tapping surfaces (trackpads, screens, smart glass frames, earbud stems).
*   **Spatial/Kinematic:** Motion, tilt, and gesture tracking via gyros/accelerometers (wands, smart rings, air mice).
*   **Optical/Vision:** Camera-based hand/body tracking, laser pointers, eye-tracking.
*   **Acoustic/Audio:** Voice commands via microphones (hearables, pendants, smart speakers).
*   **Biometric/Neural:** Muscle reading (EMG wristbands), brain-computer interfaces (EEG headbands).

**4. By Wearability**
*   **Non-Wearable (Desk/Hand):** Mouse, keyboard, tablet, standard remote, gamepad.
*   **Finger/Hand:** Smart rings, smart gloves, finger-worn trackpads.
*   **Wrist/Arm:** Smartwatches, fitness trackers, gesture bands.
*   **Neck/Torso:** Smart necklaces, lanyard microphones/cameras, smart clothing.
*   **Head/Face/Ear:** Smart glasses, hearables, head-trackers, neural headbands.
*   **Foot:** Foot pedals, smart shoes/socks.
